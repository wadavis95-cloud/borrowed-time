      One clock. Two jobs.

      Your countdown is both your time to escape and your ability budget. Every purchase takes seconds off that same clock. You run automatically; dodge, hop and choose when an advantage is worth the time.

      • Switch lanes. Press A / D or ← / →.
      • Hop small rubble (single chevron). Press W or ↑.
      • Spend 5 seconds to boost. Press Q (or 1).
      • Spend 4 seconds to leap (double chevron). Press E (or 2). Time the peak of your jump over tall barriers; clear gaps and rubble too.
      • Watch the amber markers. Move out of the marked lane before impact; hop the wreckage afterward.
      • Find your way into daylight. Time gates restore seconds along the route.
      Create a room and share its code to race with up to three friends. Vote for a map in the lobby. Most votes wins; ties are random. Your character and role name are assigned automatically. The host starts the race. Online races keep running when you switch tabs; solo practice can be paused with P or Escape.

      The four-player demo uses simulated runners connected to the same race server. You can leave it at any time and play your own run.
