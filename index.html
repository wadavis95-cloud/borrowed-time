    MAKE IT OUT

    Your time is your ticket out.

    You run forward automatically, picking up speed as you escape. Reach daylight before your clock runs out.

    • Switch lanes. Use ← / →, A / D, the buttons, or swipe sideways.
    • Hop small rubble. Press SPACE, tap the course, or swipe up.
    • Spend 3 seconds to boost. Press 1 or tap Speed boost to break away.
    • Spend 2 seconds to leap. Press 2 or tap Long jump. Time the peak of your jump over tall barriers; clear gaps and rubble too.
    • Watch the amber markers. Move out of the lane before falling stone lands. You can hop the rubble afterward.
    • Find your way into daylight. Time gates restore seconds along the route.
    Create a room and share its code to race with up to three friends. Each runner gets a different jacket color automatically. The host starts the race. Online races keep running when you switch tabs; solo practice can be paused with P or Escape.

    The four-player demo uses simulated runners connected to the same race server. You can leave it at any time and play your own run.
